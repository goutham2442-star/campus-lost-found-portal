How CampusFind
works.

A reliable, structured bridge between students who lose items, honest finders, and campus security staff to ensure safe handovers.

STEP 01
✎

Report with Details

Submit a lost or found report with item name, category, campus location, and optional photo. Our real-time Duplicate Alert System warns you if an identical item was already registered.

STEP 02
🔍

Browse & Search

Students can query the database by keywords, specific university buildings, categories, or dates. Advanced filters allow searching in real time across all active listings.

STEP 03
⚖️

Claim & Administrative Verification

Recognize your belonging? Click 'Claim' to explain unique ownership proof (serial number, internal contents, lock screens). Campus Security verifies ownership and marks the item returned.

Frequently asked questions

How does the Duplicate Item Alert System help me?

When creating a new report, CampusFind analyzes your item name, location, and category against opposite listings (e.g. comparing a new lost report against active found items) to instantly show potential matches, preventing duplicated efforts.

Can anyone claim a found item?

Only authenticated students can submit claims. Claimants must provide verifiable ownership proofs (identifying markings, stickers, serials) that are reviewed by the campus administrator before items are released.

Where can I track my submitted reports and claims?

Under your personal 'My Activity' dashboard, you can track live statuses of all items you posted as well as incoming and outgoing recovery claims.

What should I do if I find a high-value item?

Submit a found report on CampusFind, leave out one unique identifying detail (to test potential claimants), and deposit the physical item at the Campus Security Desk.
