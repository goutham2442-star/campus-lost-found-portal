How CampusFind Works

A simple step-by-step guide to reporting, finding, and claiming items on campus.

1. Report an Item

Lost something or found someone's item? Fill out the report form with the category, location, and a description. Our duplicate alert system lets you know if a similar report already exists.

2. Search & Browse

Use the search bar or filters to look up lost and found items by building, date, or category. You can view all photos and specific details on the item details page.

3. Claim & Recover

Found your item on the board? Click 'Claim' and describe identifying details (serial number, scratches, stickers). The campus administrator verifies your claim before handover.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can submit a claim?

Any registered Sai University student can submit a claim for an item marked as 'Found'.

How does the duplicate alert work?

When typing an item title and location, the system compares your words with existing items so you don't file a duplicate report.

What happens when my claim is approved?

You will receive a notification and can collect the item from the Campus Security Desk.
